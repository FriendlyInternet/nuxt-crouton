## Hypothesis
**We think that** binder links and menus should be correct whatever the page structure and language. **We'll know by** a nested binder linking correctly, groups showing names, and the menu in order and translated.

## 👤 What this is about
**Narrowed after review to nested binder links.** A binder page under a parent links its items without the parent (`/lessons/<id>` instead of `/course/lessons/<id>`) → 404.

Split out: #4449 (group names), #4450 (menu order and language).

## 🧪 What the prototype did — assume it is wrong or unclean
A local, unpushed prototype (a four-language course site in `pocs/`) proved this need with quick spike code. Treat the following as a **sketch to challenge**, not an implementation to copy:

- Worked around in the app: moved the binder to top level, dropped grouping, used series pages in the menu instead of items.
