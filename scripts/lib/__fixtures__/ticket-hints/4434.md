## Hypothesis
**We think that** each language of a video should be able to carry its own subtitle file, shown in the page's language by default. **We'll know by** French subtitles switching on by default on /fr.

## 👤 What this is about
There were no subtitles anywhere. #4376 covers the page Video block.

## Related
- #4376 (subtitles on the video block).

## 🧪 What the prototype did — assume it is wrong or unclean
A local, unpushed prototype (a four-language course site in `pocs/`) proved this need with quick spike code. Treat the following as a **sketch to challenge**, not an implementation to copy:

- For lessons (app code): `translations.<lang>.subtitles` = asset id; the player renders one `<track>` per language and turns the page-language track on itself (WebKit ignores `default` depending on system caption settings), also after hydration.
- **Likely problems:** app-level only; the page Video block untouched; per-item JS to force track modes.
